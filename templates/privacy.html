Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the Service, we also store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect more information than is needed to provide the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate core features of Spendly — logging expenses, generating category breakdowns and monthly summaries, and keeping your account secure. We may also use aggregated, anonymized data to understand usage patterns and improve the Service. We do not use your personal expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in a database associated with your account and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data will be removed within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal or security purposes.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal expense data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted third-party providers for infrastructure needs such as hosting and email delivery, who process data only on our behalf and under appropriate confidentiality obligations.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach us at support@spendly.app.
